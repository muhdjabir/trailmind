I absolutely adore Hoi An. It’s a very special place that I’ve been lucky enough to visit three times since 2009. I’ve watched it grow in popularity over the years, and it’s amazing to see how much has changed (and how much has stayed the same). The historic Ancient Town is the heart of Hoi An, a place that blends French colonial, Japanese and Chinese architecture in the most picturesque setting. It’s a bustle of energy by day that’s illuminated with color in the evenings when the Thu Bon River lights up with lanterns and floating wooden boats. Below is my complete travel guide to Hoi An including the best historical sights, rice fields, coffee shops, rooftops, day trips, restaurants, cafes, Hoi An photo locations, night markets and more.

**ANCIENT TOWN**

Hoi An Ancient Town might be my favorite place in all of Vietnam. The UNESCO World Heritage Site is an extremely well-preserved confluence of Chinese, Japanese and European architecture. The result is a vibrant labyrinth of streets lined with yellow buildings, tiled rooftops and colorful lanterns that feels like a step back in time. The two main streets in town are also the most photogenic – Trần Phú and Nguyễn Thái Học.

**ASSEMBLY HALLS**

There are five assembly halls in Hoi An that were built during the 18th and 19th centuries. They were originally built for Chinese congregations, and burst with cultural details including swallowtail roofs, yin-yang tiles, dragon carvings, bonsai trees, courtyards and incense wreaths. The prettiest of the assembly halls are Phuc Kien and Quang Dong.

**CAM CHAU RICE FIELDS**

Escape the bustle of Ancient Town with a visit to the picturesque Cam Chau rice fields. The vibrant paddies are a short distance from town accessible via a 10-minute bicycle ride or 20-minute stroll. Narrow paths of dirt form a walking labyrinth through the rice fields where farmers and water buffalo spend their days working the fields.

**CAM THANH RICE FIELDS**

Cam Thanh is another set of beautiful rice fields located just outside of Ancient Town. The sea of green is breathtaking, and offers a nice change of pace from the chaos of the river, cafes and markets. It’s a 20-minute bicycle ride from town to reach the rice fields, or a 10-minute taxi ride.

**CUA DAI BEACH**

Cua Dai Beach sits just a few kilometers north of Ancient Town. The powder white sand and calm blue waters offer up an ideal setting to relax in the afternoon after a morning of sightseeing. Luxury resorts line the beach, as well as sun beds for rent, seafood stalls and restaurants.

**HOI AN NIGHT MARKET**

Open seven days a week from 5 PM through 10 PM, the Hoi An Night Market lines Nguyen Hoang Street where dozens of stalls light up the streets selling colorful glowing lanterns, street food, juices, clothing, trinkets, jewelry and more. It’s a must-visit while in town.

**JAPANESE COVERED BRIDGE**

Chua Cau is the iconic Japanese Covered Bridge that crosses a tiny canal off the Hoi An River. The ornate wooden footbridge dates back to the 16th century and features a pink tiled rooftop, animal statues and a mix of Chinese, Japanese and Vietnamese design cues.

**THU BON RIVER**

While in Hoi An it’s hard to miss the beautiful Thu Bon River that borders Ancient Town. During the day, the bank of merchant buildings bathed in yellow serve up a stunning backdrop for the river. At night, the river transforms into a magical kaleidoscope of color thanks to the vibrant glowing lanterns that line the river and hang from the floating wooden boats. My favorite photo location in the evenings is on the cement blocks that line the river, near the Night Market.

In the Vietnamese culture, the color yellow represents prosperity, happiness and harmony.

**CHAM ISLAND** **ARCHIPELAGO**

The beautiful Cham Island Archipelago is a group of eight islands located off the coast of Hoi An. A UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, the main island Hon Lao has become a popular tourist attraction in recent years with ferry and speed boat transfers available from Cua Dai Pier, in Hoi An. Transfer time takes 30 to 45 minutes. Hon Lao is home to beaches, turquoise water and offshore snorkeling, but the setting isn’t comparable to the islands in Thailand. There are a handful of casual places to eat on the island, as well as local homestays and guesthouses.

**MY SON SANCTUARY**

The My Son Sanctuary is a mini-Angkor Wat located just 90 minutes from Hoi An. It sits in a lush green valley below Cat’s Tooth Mountain. The ancient temples date back to the 4th and 13th centuries, composed primarily of red fired brick and sandstone. My Son is a UNESCO World Heritage Site that is a popular tour group destination, but it’s more enjoyable if you come on your own and see the ruins at your own pace.

The My Son cham temple ruins are my favorite day trip from Hoi An.

**FOUR SEASONS RESORT THE NAM HAI**

Not only is Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai the nicest place to stay in Hoi An, it’s also one of the best resorts in all of Vietnam. The property is an exquisite blend of modern luxury and cultural design that features water gardens, tiered infinity pools, platform luxury villas and a resort layout inspired by the garden houses of Hue. There are a variety of villas to choose from – beachfront, oceanfront and pool villas. All are overly spacious with plush king beds, sunken desks, a lounge area, deep tubs and gorgeous cultural design details. This is my favorite place to stay in Hoi An.

Moire Hoi An is a fine alternative to the Four Seasons for travelers looking for something new, contemporary and high-end without the $$$$ price tag. It’s a new property that opened in 2024 and the design is airy, bright and sophisticated. The beautiful exterior has been designed entirely in yellow with white window shutters to pay homage to the architecture of Ancient Town. The rooms here feel very clean, new and modern. Moire Hoi An sits on the banks of the Do River, an easy 10-minute stroll to Ancient Town.

Situated on the Thu Bon River just a few miles from Ancient Town, Namia River Retreat offers 60 luxurious villas in a tranquil wellness setting. The villas are stunning and boast private pools, sunken tubs and epic views of the river. This place is gorgeous, and an absolute vibe.
